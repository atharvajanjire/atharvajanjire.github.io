Thesis

Graduation Project · 2025

Identifying a Revenue Model for Gravity Gardens to Ensure Financial Stability

Van Hall Larenstein University of Applied Sciences · International Agri-Business & Trade

A strategic research project designed to build a viable revenue model for Gravity Gardens — a Dutch Agri-tech startup specialising in hyper-gravity seed priming and molecular farming. Combined the ISADA market-selection framework, thematic analysis, and the Revenue Model Canvas by Jan Jonker to recommend a hybrid monetisation strategy aligned with EU seed companies.

▸ 12 expert interviews across seed companies, Agri-tech firms, and molecular farming.
▸ ISADA framework applied to identify EU seed companies as the optimal segment.
▸ Hybrid revenue model proposed: equipment sales + subscriptions + performance pricing.
▸ SDG alignment: mapped the solution to SDGs 2, 9, 12, 13, and 17.
▸ 2 stakeholders converted into strategic partners, cutting R&D timeline by ~12 months.
▸ Commissioner: Robin Le Vigouroux (COO, Gravity Gardens).
